Construction / Flat fills

Hard edges.

The app uses 2 dp ink outlines and hard shadows, never blur. This site uses 3 px borders and offset shadows. Buttons press into their shadows on hover and activation.

Pastel fills carry dark ink for contrast. The app follows the phone's theme; this site follows your browser's preferred colour scheme.

The Compose kit

ui/theme/Theme.kt defines NeoPalette, exposed as Neo.palette, Material 3 colour mapping, shapes, and the type scale. Pastel fills always use dark onAccent ink.

ui/neo/Neo.kt contains the shared components.
